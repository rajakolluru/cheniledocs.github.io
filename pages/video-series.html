Production notes for the whole series
Keep a consistent cold-open (5–8s brand sting), lower-thirds for terms (service policy, last mile, ChenileExchange), and a recurring on-screen diagram that gains one layer per episode so the architecture literally assembles as the story progresses. Every episode ends with the same one-line recap card and a pointer to the matching page on this site.
